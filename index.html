About Dave.

Cologne, Germany
50° 56' N · 6° 57' E

I'm Dave Rindo Rindo — Art Director with 7+ years across design, illustration and visual brand communication. From gaming and automotive to lifestyle, I craft concepts with a strong sense for brand and audience.

After completing my studies in Product Design, I gained over six years of experience in art direction, graphic design, and project management for both national and international clients. My professional journey began at Oliver Schrott Kommunikation (OSK), where I worked, among others, for Daimler/Mercedes. There, I designed trade show and event architectures, illustrated storyboards, and produced high-quality renderings.

Before my sabbatical in 2024, I worked at Build a Rocket on campaigns for KitKat, Porsche, LG and SAP. I'm fluent in Adobe CC, Cinema 4D, Blender, Figma and Procreate — and integrate AI tools like ChatGPT, Midjourney and Runway into my workflows.
